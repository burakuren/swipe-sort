S07 Profile, connected accounts and account deletion

Pushed from Settings (S06) in the Settings tab stack. Edit name and photo, manage sign-in methods (Google, Apple on iOS, email and password) and delete the account with a clear account of what is deleted and what stays. Spec: S07-profile.md.

Profile

Name and photo are what other members see. Email is the sign-in address and is read-only. Sign-in methods list every way into the account.

Sign-in methods

Connect or disconnect Google (and Apple on iOS); add or change the password. A method can be disconnected only while another one remains.

Delete account

Required by both app stores. Step 1 explains what is deleted and what stays and asks what happens to shared lists the user owns; step 2 asks to type "delete"; a final dialog confirms. Sensitive steps may ask to sign in again first.

Offline, iOS, web and large text

Offline: everything is readable, changes are disabled with the reason. iOS adds Sign in with Apple to sign-in methods and uses grouped inset lists with a back chevron. Web uploads a photo file instead of camera or gallery.
