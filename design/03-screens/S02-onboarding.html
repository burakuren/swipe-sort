S02 Onboarding

First launch only (replay from Settings › Help). Three steps: welcome, a hands-on gesture demo with neutral sample cards, and a recap. No account is ever required (ACC-01). The demo uses the same drag feedback as S25 (tilt, tint, stamp, armed button); physics in motion.md §2 and live in swipe-demo.html. Spec: S02-onboarding.md.

Welcome

What SwipeSort does in one sentence, shown with real sample cards. "Get started" opens the demo; "I have an account" opens S03. Skip goes straight to an empty Lists screen (S08 first run).

Gesture demo

A deck of four neutral sample cards (products and photos from sample data). The user tries all three gestures in any order; the checklist fills as each one is used, and the instruction always names the next untried gesture. Buttons count too. Nothing is saved. After the third gesture the recap opens automatically (700 ms after the commit).

Recap

The three sample decisions, shown as decision badges in the Reject, Maybe, Accept order, then into the app. "Get started" opens Lists in its first-run state (S08) without asking for an account.

Accessibility

Reduced motion: the card follows the finger without tilt and the hint arrow is static; tint and stamp still show. Large text: the welcome illustration hides and the demo uses the labeled button grid from S25. Screen readers: the card exposes Accept, Reject and Maybe actions, and the instruction says so.

iOS and web

iOS: navigation bar with a back chevron and Skip as a text button; edge swipe goes back a step. Web: same layout; mobile browsers show a short note about the app; desktop browsers add arrow-key hints (web only).
