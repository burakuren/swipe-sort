S37 Bulk resolve: confirmation and summary

The Decision Maker applies their own earlier decisions to every open conflict in the current round in one action (DM-11). Conflicts on items they never decided stay open, and both the confirmation and the summary say how many (DM-12). Spec: S37-bulk-resolve.md.

Entry and confirmation

Mert reaches bulk resolve from the conflicts screen ("Resolve all") or from the list overview's conflicts row, where only the Decision Maker sees this choice.

Edge cases

Nothing to resolve, partial failure, undo, offline and members without the role.

Accessibility and iOS

Large text keeps the counts and the open items readable; the sheet scrolls. iOS: the confirmation is a page sheet with Cancel in the bar.
