S34 Members and roles

Who is on the list, how far each member is, who resolves conflicts, and every member-management action: invite, change roles, assign the Decision Maker with one switch (DM-10), remove, transfer ownership, leave. Spec: S34-members.md.

Owner view

Deniz (Owner) on Wedding guests. Mert is the Decision Maker. The Decision Maker group is the single place to assign the role: one switch per member, at most one on.

Decision Maker switch (DM-10)

One tap on a member's switch. Moving the role from one member to another asks once, because it changes who resolves the open conflicts. Turning it on when nobody has the role applies immediately.

Member options

Tap a member's options button (or the row). The sheet repeats the Decision Maker switch (same state as the group), and holds Make Owner and Remove from list.

Leaving

Members can leave at any time; their decisions stay. The Owner must hand the list over first.

Other members' views

Collaborators and the Decision Maker see the same screen read-only: no switches or member options. They can invite as Collaborator and leave.

States

Loading, error and offline. Offline, the screen shows the last synced progress; every change is disabled with the reason.

Accessibility

Large text: names and role badges wrap, counts move under the progress bars, the switch rows grow.

iOS and web

iOS: back chevron with the list name, inset grouped sections, iOS switches, member options as an action sheet. Web desktop: the phone layout centered; member options open as a dropdown menu next to the button.
