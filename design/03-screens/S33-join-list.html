S33 Join a list

The signed-in invited person sees who invited them, the list, their role and what it means, the members and their progress, then joins and starts sorting every item of the current round (DM-16). Spec: S33-join-list.md.

Join

Selin joins from the app (Android). Joining takes one tap; sorting starts right away.

Web

Prototype flow 3: after signing in on the desktop browser (S03-web-desktop), Mert joins and sorts right away with keyboard shortcuts. Nothing to install; "Get the app" is offered but secondary.

States

Loading the invite details, offline, the link reset while the person was deciding, and a list with no round in progress.

Accessibility and iOS

Large text: the role rows grow, progress counts move below their bars, the action stays pinned. iOS: full-screen modal with Cancel in the navigation bar.
