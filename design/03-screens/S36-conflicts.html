S36 Conflicts: one-by-one resolution

Swipe through conflicts with every member's decision and notes on the card. The Decision Maker sets the final decision with the same gestures and buttons as sorting (S25); other members see what is open and can change their own decision. Spec: S36-conflicts.md.

Decision Maker: one by one

Mert is the Decision Maker under the rule Decision Maker decides. Right = Accept, left = Reject, up = Maybe, like sorting, but the result is the final decision. "Resolve all" applies his earlier decisions in one step (S37).

Other rules and members

What members who are not resolving see, and how the screen works under the other two rules. Members always see every decision and the shared notes, and can change their own decision.

States

Empty, loading, error and offline. Resolutions are written on the phone first and synced; conflicts can change during sync.

Accessibility

At 200% text the card shows the name, the status and the decisions only (notes move to item details) and the buttons become a labeled grid. Reduced motion: no tilt or fly-out; tint and stamp still show.

iOS and web

iOS: pushed screen with back chevron, UIMenu for options, page sheet for notes. Web desktop: phone layout centered with the same keyboard keys as sorting.
