S35 Conflict rule settings

How a conflict gets its final decision on a shared list: Everyone must agree, Majority decides or Decision Maker decides (DM-06 to DM-09). The Owner chooses; everyone else sees what the rule means for them. Spec: S35-conflict-rule.md.

Owner

The Owner picks one of three rules. Each option explains itself with the same example item and says what switching does to the 15 open conflicts in Round 1. Switching asks for confirmation with the recalculation.

Without a Decision Maker

Majority without a Decision Maker leaves ties open. Decision Maker decides cannot work without one, so picking it asks the Owner to choose a member first, with the same switch as Members (S34).

Members who are not the Owner

Read-only: the current rule, who chose it and what it means for this member. The Decision Maker gets a direct way to the conflicts.

States

Loading, offline and a change that could not be saved.

Accessibility and iOS

At 200% text the cards grow and stay stacked. iOS: pushed screen with a back chevron; confirmations are native alerts with the same copy.
