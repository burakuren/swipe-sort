S06 Settings

The Settings tab and every sub-page: profile entry, backup, card templates, appearance, haptics, notifications, language, privacy, help and feedback, about, sign out. Signed-in and not-signed-in variants, offline, large text, iOS and web. Spec: S06-settings.md.

Settings root

Top-level tab (bottom navigation: Lists, Activity, Settings). Groups are flat card surfaces with inset rows; the profile row opens S07. Haptics is a switch right on the root: one control does not need its own page.

Preferences

Appearance (theme), notifications and language. Changes apply immediately; there is no Save button.

Backup

Signed-in lists back up automatically whenever the phone is online. Backup states: backed up, backing up, paused (failed or offline), and not backed up (no account).

Privacy, help and about

Plain-language privacy information, help, feedback, version and licenses. External pages open in the in-app browser (marked with an external-link icon).

Accessibility

At 200% font size rows grow, values move under the title instead of beside it, and nothing truncates except the email address.

iOS

Inset grouped lists, a large title that collapses on scroll, the iOS tab bar, and back chevrons with the previous title (edge swipe goes back). Sign out confirms with an action sheet.

Web

Same layout in the browser. Haptics stay visible but unavailable, with the reason and Get the app. Notifications are in-app only. Desktop shows the phone layout centered.
