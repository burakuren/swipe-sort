S03 Sign up / sign in

Auth modal stack. Google and email on every platform; Sign in with Apple first on iOS (HIG). Email has a sign-in form and a create-account form; "Forgot password?" opens S04. Signing in is never required to use SwipeSort alone (ACC-01). Provider marks are stand-ins for the official assets (spec §8). Spec: S03-sign-in.md.

Choose a way to sign in

Android and web: Google, then email. The context line appears when S03 opens from a share or backup prompt (S05 › Continue with email).

Email

One sign-in form and one create-account form; each links to the other. Password rules are shown live while typing. Errors sit under the field they concern, or above the form when they concern the account.

Errors

What happened and how to fix it. No apologies, no error codes. Typed values are kept.

Google

Google opens the system account chooser (out of scope). While it returns, the Google button shows progress and the other options are disabled. Cancelling returns quietly; a failure explains what to do.

Offline and large text

Offline: sign-in buttons are disabled with the reason; the user can close and keep working without an account. Large text: the mark hides, buttons grow and wrap.

iOS

Presented as a page sheet with Cancel. Sign in with Apple comes first (App Store guideline 4.8 and HIG), then Google, then email. The Apple button is the system AppleAuthenticationButton.

Web

Mobile browsers: the Android layout without Apple. Desktop browsers: the phone column, used by an invited member in prototype flow 3 (Mert opens Deniz's link and signs in to join).
