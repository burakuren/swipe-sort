S10 List management

Rename, duplicate, archive, delete and leave, from the list's overflow menu (S09) or a long press on a row (S08); plus the Archived lists screen. Who sees what depends on the role: the Owner manages the list; members can duplicate and leave. Spec: S10-list-management.md.

Actions menu

The overflow menu on S09 (Android DropdownMenu, anchored to the button). Items depend on the role: Owner of a solo or shared list: Rename, Duplicate, Archive, Leave (shared only), Delete list. Collaborator and Decision Maker: Duplicate, Leave list. A long press on a Home row opens the same actions as a bottom sheet. iOS uses an action sheet.

Rename

A dialog with the name selected. Names are 1 to 80 characters; Save stays disabled while the name is empty or too long, and the error says how to fix it. Duplicate names are allowed (lists have IDs).

Duplicate

Duplicate makes a new list from the same items. Choose a fresh start (items only) or keep my decisions and notes. A shared list always duplicates into a new solo list owned by me; other members, their decisions and their notes stay in the shared list. The copy opens right away.

Archive

Archive hides a list from Lists without deleting anything. A solo list archives at once with Undo. Archiving a shared list affects every member, so the Owner confirms; members keep read-only access.

Delete

Delete is Owner only and always confirms. The dialog names the list, says exactly what goes and what stays safe (sources are never touched). After deleting, Home shows "List deleted" with Undo for 6 seconds; the deletion is committed when the snackbar closes. Deleting a shared list deletes it for every member, with stronger copy and a specific button.

Leave

Members (Collaborator, Decision Maker) can leave a shared list. Their decisions stay and still count, so other members' results don't shift unexpectedly; items they haven't decided stop waiting for them; their private notes are deleted. The Owner can't leave until someone else is the Owner.

Archived lists

Reached from the "Archived lists" row at the end of Home. Rows open the list read-only (S09-archived). Each row's menu: Open, Restore, Duplicate, Delete forever. Restoring puts the list back in Lists exactly as it was.

Offline

What works offline: Rename, Duplicate, Archive and Restore of solo lists, and Delete of a solo list (all saved on the phone and synced later). What needs a connection: anything that changes a shared list for other members (Archive, Delete, Leave). Those items stay visible, disabled, with the reason.

Accessibility, iOS and web

Large text: dialog buttons stack full width. iOS: rows in Archived lists use swipe actions (Restore, Delete) in addition to the menu. Web: menus and dialogs behave like Android; on desktop the dialog sits in the phone column.
