S20 Duplicate review

Step 4 of the import: items that look alike are shown side by side with matching fields marked by icon and label. Merge, keep both or skip; anything not reviewed is kept. Also used when adding items to an existing list (S21). Spec: S20-duplicate-review.md.

Review

Four groups from the sample data (c001/c097, c093/c098, c005/c099, c058/c100). Each group shows both items, marks matching fields with an icon and the word Same or Similar, and offers Merge, Keep both or Skip.

No duplicates and loading

Detection runs after mapping, because phone numbers and emails are compared in their mapped type. When nothing is found the step is skipped (A-23); this screen appears when the user steps back from Finish.

Against items already in the list

Used by S21 Add items: rows from the new source are compared with items already in the list. The left column is the existing item with its final decision; merging fills in values and keeps decisions and notes.

Many duplicates

Large imports collapse groups into rows with a quick action each, a filter by match type and bulk actions. Tapping a row opens the full comparison.

Accessibility

At 200% text the two items no longer fit side by side: each field lists one value when both are the same, or both values numbered 1 and 2. Options stack as full-width buttons.

iOS

Same review layout with the iOS bar (back chevron, edge swipe goes back to mapping). The merge detail is a page sheet with Cancel and Merge in its bar instead of bottom buttons.
