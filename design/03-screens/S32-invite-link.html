S32 Opening an invite link

What the invited person sees when they open swipesort.app/join/<token>: in the app when installed (Android App Link, iOS Universal Link), otherwise in the web app, where they can sign in, join and sort without installing anything. Spec: S32-invite-link.md.

Web app (app not installed)

Prototype flow 3: Mert opens the link Deniz copied, on his laptop. The page is public (no sign-in needed to see it) but shows only what the invited person needs: who invited them, the list, their role and the members.

Default: app installed, not signed in

The link opens the app. Without an account (or as a guest) the app shows the same invite preview as the web page; "Sign in to join" opens S03 and returns to S33. Device lists move to the account on sign-in (S05 moving step).

App installed

The link opens SwipeSort directly. A short loading state names the list, then the join screen (S33) replaces it. iOS behaves the same with a Universal Link (no separate frame).

Links that no longer work

Expired, reset and unknown links never show the list's contents. When the link can still be traced to its sender (expired or reset), the page names them so the person knows whom to ask.

Role changed

A Decision Maker invite works for one person. If someone else took the role first (or the Owner changed it), the page says so before the person signs in.
