S18 Photos import

Step 2 of the photos import: select photos from the gallery (Android, iOS) or upload photo files (web). Each photo becomes one item with fixed fields (Photo, Taken, Location, File name), so field mapping is skipped. Spec: S18-photos-import.md.

Gallery selection (Android, iOS)

After photo access is granted (S12-photos), SwipeSort shows its own selection grid: grouped by day, newest first, 4 columns. Tap a photo to select it; the day checkbox selects the whole day. Each photo becomes one item.

iOS

Same grid on iOS with full access (iOS bar: back chevron, centered title, close at the right). With limited access, the grid shows only the photos the person shared, with a way to share more.

Web: upload photo files

The gallery picker needs the Android or iOS app (PLT-08). On the web the same step uploads photo files instead: JPG, PNG, HEIC or WebP, up to 25 MB each, up to 5,000 per import. Format and size are checked before upload; files that can't be added are listed with the reason.
