S38 Activity and notifications

The Activity tab: what happened on shared lists, grouped by day, with unread state and filters. Plus how SwipeSort notifications read on the phone, the in-app banner, web in-app notifications, notification preferences and the push pre-permission. Spec: S38-activity.md.

Activity feed

Deniz (Owner of Wedding guests). Six event types: someone joined, finished sorting, new conflicts, conflicts resolved, a new round started, a role changed. Each row opens the screen it is about.

States

Empty, loading, error and offline.

Notifications

Push asks for permission only after a list is shared or joined (S12). Activity always shows every event; preferences decide which ones also push. Notifications turned off: the feed explains and links to the fix.

Web

Push notifications are not available on web (PLT-10): Activity and the badge update live while the tab is open, new events also show as a toast, and the feed explains why push needs the app.

Accessibility and iOS

Large text: rows grow, times move under the text. iOS: large title, standard tab bar.
