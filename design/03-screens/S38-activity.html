Notifications

Push asks for permission only after a list is shared or joined (S12). Activity always shows every event; preferences decide which ones also push. Notifications turned off: the feed explains and links to the fix.

Web

Push notifications are not available on web (PLT-10): Activity and the badge update live while the tab is open, new events also show as a toast, and the feed explains why push needs the app.
