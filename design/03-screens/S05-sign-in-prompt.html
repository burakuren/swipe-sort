S05 Sign-in prompt and moving lists

A bottom sheet over the screen that needs an account: Share or Invite on a list (S09, S10), Back up (S06, S08 indicator). It explains why, signs in, then moves every list on this phone to the account and continues the original action. Backgrounds are simplified, non-interactive copies of S09 and S08. Spec: S05-sign-in-prompt.md.

Why sign in

Opened when someone without an account taps Share, Invite or Back up. The sheet names the reason, says what happens to their lists, and offers Google and email (Apple first on iOS). "Not now" closes it; nothing changes. Continue with email opens S03 with the same context line.

Moving lists to the account

Right after sign-in, every list on this phone moves to the account, one by one, newest activity first. Each list stays usable locally the whole time. When the move finishes, the original action continues: Share opens the invite (S31); Back up returns to the backup page (S06).

Move failures

Lists are never deleted from the phone until the account confirms them. A partial failure lets sharing continue when the shared list itself moved; a full failure retries automatically when the connection returns.

Large text, iOS and web

Large text: the reasons list hides, buttons grow and the sheet becomes full height. iOS: a page sheet with grabber, Sign in with Apple first. Web: lists made without an account live in this browser only ("On this browser only"); Apple is not offered.
