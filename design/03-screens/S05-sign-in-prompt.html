S05 Sign-in prompt and moving lists

A bottom sheet over the screen that needs an account: Share or Invite on a list (S09, S10), Back up (S06, S08 indicator). It explains why, signs in, then moves the lists on this phone to the account (all by default; Choose lists keeps some on this phone only, D-33) and continues the original action. The sample list never moves (D-10). Backgrounds are simplified, non-interactive copies of S09 and S08. Spec: S05-sign-in-prompt.md.

Why sign in

Opened when someone without an account taps Share, Invite or Back up. The sheet names the reason, says what happens to their lists (with "Choose lists" to keep some on this phone), and offers Google, Apple and email (Apple first on iOS, Google first on Android and web, A-140). "Not now" closes it; nothing changes. Continue with email opens S03 with the same context line.

Choose lists

Every list on this phone moves by default. "Choose lists" opens a checklist inside the same sheet: all lists start checked, the list being shared is locked on ("Needed to share"), and the sample list is never listed (D-10). Unchecked lists stay on this phone only and belong to the account (D-33, A-135). The frames show "Frontend hiring, Q4" unchecked.

Moving lists to the account

Right after sign-in, the lists on this phone move to the account, one by one, the shared list first, then newest activity. Lists left unchecked in Choose lists show "On this phone only" and never upload. Each list stays usable locally the whole time. When the move finishes, the original action continues: Share opens the invite (S31); Back up returns to the backup page (S06).

Move failures

Lists are never deleted from the phone until the account confirms them. A partial failure lets sharing continue when the shared list itself moved; a full failure retries automatically when the connection returns.

Large text, iOS and web

Large text: the sheet becomes full height; the reasons stay and the text scrolls above pinned buttons. iOS: a page sheet with grabber, Sign in with Apple first; Choose lists uses a navigation bar with Back and Done and round iOS checkmarks. Web: lists made without an account live in this browser only ("On this browser only"), and the copy says browsers can clear lists not opened for a while (D-08); Apple is offered through Apple's sign-in page (D-13).
