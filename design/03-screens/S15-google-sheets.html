S15 Google Sheets connection and sheet/tab selection

Select items for the Google Sheets source: connect read-only, pick a spreadsheet, choose one or more tabs, preview, then map fields. Spec: S15-google-sheets.md. Shared import chrome (modal app bar, ImportStepper, bottom actions) matches S16–S21.

Connect

Google Sheets needs its own read-only permission, even when you signed in to SwipeSort with Google. No SwipeSort account is required. The Google account chooser is native (out of scope).

Choose a spreadsheet

Recently edited first, including spreadsheets shared with you. Search filters as you type.

Choose tabs and preview

One tab, or several with the same columns combined into one list. Combined tabs add a Tab field so items keep their origin. Empty tabs are listed but can't be selected.

Errors and offline

Google connection failure (SRC-23), revoked access and no connection. Every state keeps a way to another source.

Web and iOS

Desktop and mobile web use a Google popup (OAuth). iOS uses the system web sign-in sheet (out of scope), then the same screens in a page sheet.
