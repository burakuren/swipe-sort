S27 Round summary

The end of a round: my counts, what happened to the list and the next actions (review results, re-sort Maybe, start a new round, export). Spec: S27-round-summary.md.

Solo list

S27 replaces S25 after the last decision (500 ms after the commit, success haptic). One orchestrated moment: the three counts count up once (500 ms). Counts follow the decision order Reject, Maybe, Accept.

Shared list

My round is finished; the list is not. The summary shows my counts, the list status (Agreed, Pending, Conflict) and who is still sorting. The Decision Maker sees Review conflicts as the main action; only the Owner starts rounds.

Accessibility

At 200% text the counts become labeled rows and the title left-aligns. Screen readers hear one announcement: "Round 1 complete. 24 rejected, 14 maybe, 62 accepted." The count-up is not announced.

iOS and web

iOS: Done sits in the navigation bar (no close icon); the summary replaces the sorting modal without a slide. Web: same layout; on desktop Enter runs the main action.
