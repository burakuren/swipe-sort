S25 Sorting screen

The heart of the product and the quality reference for every other screen. Spec: S25-sorting.md. Live interaction: swipe-demo.html. Motion values: motion.md.

Sorting

The card stack with progress, decision buttons and counts. Gestures: right Accept, left Reject, up Maybe. Values in motion.md §2.

First-time coach marks

Shown on the first sort only. The card demonstrates each gesture (static arrows with reduced motion). Skip ends the tutorial; it can be replayed from the menu.

Notes and menu

Add or edit a note without leaving the stack. Tap the card to open item details (S26).

Shared list

Every member decides independently. Others' decisions are hidden by default to avoid bias; each member can turn them on from the menu.

Any kind of list

The card follows the list's card template. No domain words in the interface: the same screen sorts products, photos and candidates.

Content edge cases

Long text clamps on the card and shows in full in details. Missing values are left out. Edited values carry a marker.

States

Loading, offline, errors, the last card and resuming.

Accessibility

Large text, reduced motion and screen-reader actions. Every gesture has a visible button and a named action.

iOS and web

iOS: modal with a top bar and an iOS page sheet for notes; swipe-down to dismiss is off while sorting. Web: no haptics; desktop shows the phone layout centered with keyboard hints.
