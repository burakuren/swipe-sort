S28 Results

Results per decision with counts, search, filters and sorting in every segment; shared lists switch between final decisions and my decisions; any decision can be changed from here. Spec: S28-results.md. Opened from S27, S09 and S30; filters in S29.

Solo list: Desk setup after Round 2

Results show the latest decision per item across rounds (A-33): Round 2 re-sorted the 6 Maybe items, so Desk setup now has 8 accepted, 1 maybe and 11 rejected. Segments lead with Accept (the result people come for) and keep icon, label and count. Rows are full-bleed: thumbnail, title, price and store, decision badge, overflow menu.

Search, filter and sort

Every segment has its own search (RES-02), filters (RES-03) and sorting (RES-04); they apply to the segment shown. Filter and Sort open the S29 sheet; applied filters appear as removable chips. Search matches the title, every visible field and notes.

Shared list: Wedding guests (Round 1 in progress)

Final decisions (RES-05) show the list-level result: Agreed items in Accept, Maybe and Reject; Conflicts (15) and Pending (53) are not final yet and get their own segments, so no item is hidden. My decisions show only my own choices, with the list status on every row so the difference from the final decision is visible. Rows show who decided what (COL-16).

Round results (opened from S30)

Tapping a round in history opens Results for that round: the decisions as they were when the round ended. Rows say what changed later. Search, filters and sorting work the same; Export always uses the latest results.

Loading, offline, error and long lists

Results come from the working copy on the phone, so loading is short and offline still works; shared statuses update after sync. Long lists keep the segment bar pinned and add a fast-scroll handle.

iOS, web and large text

iOS: native back with the list name, swipe actions on rows (trailing: the other two decisions; leading: Note), context menu on long press. Web: same layout; desktop shows keyboard hints and opens the row menu as a popover. Large text: rows reflow, the badge moves under the title.
