S26 Item detail, editing and notes

All fields of one item, my decision, who decided what on shared lists, notes, and editing in place with revert. Spec: S26-item-detail.md. Opened from S25 (sheet), S28 and S36 (pushed).

Item detail over sorting (bottom sheet)

Tap the card in S25 (or Enter on desktop web, or the card action "Open details"). The sheet opens at about 85% height; drag it up or use the expand button for full screen. Deciding from the sheet closes it and runs the commit on the card.

Editing fields

Edit (or tap any field) switches the fields into inputs in place. Each field type gets its own keyboard and control. Save changes writes to the working copy; changed fields are marked Edited and keep their original value. Image fields have no photo library or camera (D-32): a contact photo can only be removed, an image from a link is edited as its link.

Edited fields and revert

Edited fields carry a pencil-line Edited badge, the original value and Revert to original (SAFE-03, SAFE-04). Revert asks first and offers Undo afterwards. When a later edit by another member replaced mine at sync, the item opened from the S40 snackbar asks Keep mine / Use Mert's (D-09 row 1, A-70).

Notes

One note per member per item. On shared lists notes are visible to all members by default; a private note shows a lock, "Only you" and a dashed outline (DM-18, DM-19). Solo lists have no private option.

Shared lists: who decided what

Every item shows each member's decision with icon and label, the list status and the final decision (COL-16). While sorting with others' decisions hidden (SRT-12), the sheet keeps them hidden until I decide.

Opened from Results: change decision

Pushed from an S28 row. My decision becomes a segmented Reject, Maybe, Accept control (RES-06); a change shows a snackbar with Undo. Products and places use the same screen.

States

Loading, offline save, an item removed by another member, and read-only archived lists.

Accessibility

At 200% text, label and value stack, everything wraps and the decision buttons become full-width rows.

iOS and web

iOS: a page sheet with medium and large detents, Edit and Done in the navigation bar; while editing, Cancel and Save replace them. Web desktop: Enter opens details from S25 as the same sheet inside the centered column, with keyboard hints.
