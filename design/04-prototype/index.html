SwipeSort prototype

The four main flows end to end, plus two flows added by the design update after build planning (keep a list on this phone only; contacts through the system picker). Each step opens one frame of the design. Inside a frame, use the buttons and links to move on, or come back here and pick the next step. Two steps are live: the swipe demo sorts real sample cards with touch, mouse or the keyboard (← Reject, ↑ Maybe, → Accept, Z Undo) and then continues to the round summary. To see a step in dark mode, set data-theme="dark" on the page, or add &theme=dark to the swipe demo address. Everything else: README, handoff guide, swipe demo.
