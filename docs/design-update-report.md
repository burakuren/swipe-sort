### D-33 Keep a list on this phone only

The decisions are A-132 to A-139 (lead) with A-142–A-148, A-168, A-175–A-178, A-187. Answers to D-33's
questions:

1. **Where the switch lives and its label.** A Switch row **"Back up this list"** in its own "Backup" group at
   the end of S09 (list overview), for a signed-in Owner of a solo list. On: "Backed up to {email}". Off: "On
   this phone only". States: Backing up…, Backs up when you're online (queued), Backup paused, disabled on
   shared lists and when offline (turning off needs a connection). The same switch, on by default, sits at the
   import finish step (S22) so a private list never touches the server. S06 › Backup lists the lists on this
   phone only, each with "Back up". The list menu does not repeat the action (A-176).
   Frames: `S09-device-only`, `S09-backing-up`, `S09-backed-up`, `S09-backup-queued`, `S09-shared-backup`,
   `S09-backup-large-text`, `S09-web-device-only`, `S22-import-pick-device-only` (+ web, large text),
   `S06-backup-list-backing-up`, `S06-backup-list-backed-up`.
2. **Sharing a device-only list.** Sharing needs backup: Share/Invite opens the sheet "Back up this list to
   share it" ("Members sort a shared list on their own devices, so it needs to be in your account.") with "Back
   up and share" and "Not now"; the list backs up, then Invite opens with "“{name}” is backed up". Offline the
   sheet says sharing needs a connection. Frames `S10-share-backup`, `S10-share-backing-up`,
   `S10-share-backup-offline`.
3. **Turning backup off for a backed-up list.** Confirmation, destructive because the account copy is deleted:
   "Keep “{name}” on this phone only?" / "The copy in your account is deleted and the list leaves your other
   devices. If you lose this phone or move to a new one, the list can't be recovered." / "Cancel" / "Keep on
   this phone only". iOS uses an alert (read first). Needs a connection. Done: "“{name}” is on this phone only"
   + Undo (backs it up again). Not allowed on shared lists ("Shared lists are always backed up."); once the
   Owner has removed every member and turned off every invite link, the list is solo again and the switch
   works. Other devices of the account remove the list and say once: "You kept “{name}” on another device
   only. It's no longer on this one." Frames `S10-backup-off`, `S10-ios-backup-off`, `S10-backup-off-offline`,
   `S10-backup-off-done`, `S40-backup-off`, `S40-ios-backup-off`, `S40-outcome-device-only-elsewhere`.
4. **Sign-in.** Lists on this phone still move by default, but the S05 prompt offers "Choose lists" (all
   checked; the list being shared locked on, "Needed to share"; the sample never listed). Unchecked lists stay
   on this phone only and belong to the account. Result: "3 lists moved to your account" / "1 stays on this
   phone only…". Frames `S05-choose-lists`, `S05-share-chosen`, `S05-backup-choose-lists`,
   `S05-choose-lists-large-text`, `S05-ios-choose-lists`, `S05-web-choose-lists`, `S05-moving-some-kept`,
   `S05-moved-some-kept`.
5. **Sign-out and another account on the same phone.** Backed-up lists leave the phone (A-52). Lists on this
   phone only stay, hidden while signed out, and come back when the same account signs in on this phone.
   Another account, or using the app without one, never sees them. The sign-out dialog names them:
   "“Frontend hiring, Q4” stays on this phone, hidden until you sign in again." Frames
   `S06-sign-out-device-only`, `S06-ios-sign-out-device-only`.
6. **Labels.** For signed-in people, a list on this phone only carries "On this phone only" (`cloud-off`,
   muted) on its Home row and in its S09 header; backed-up lists show nothing. Web: "On this browser only" plus
   the D-08 line. People without an account keep the single Home indicator line. The label "On this phone only"
   and the action "Keep on this phone only" are fixed terms on iOS too; iOS sentences say "this iPhone" (A-137).
   A list on this phone only appears only on the device that holds it (not in the web app).
7. **Account deletion and new phone.** Lists on this phone only are not in the account, so deleting the
   account doesn't delete them: S07 lists them under "Not changed" and says "“Frontend hiring, Q4” stays on
   this phone"; afterwards they are lists without an account (Done opens `S08-guest`). A new phone never
   receives them: the turn-off confirmation and the S06 › Backup / S09 footer say "Lists on this phone only don't
   move to a new phone. Back them up first."

## Feedback on decisions

Applied as decided; arguments for the founder:

- **D-32 and single images.** "No photo-library access at all" also removes adding one picture to a manual
  item or replacing a contact photo. The system Photo Picker (Android Photo Picker, iOS PHPicker) needs no
  permission, so it meets the least-privilege rule (ADR-0005). Pasting an image link on a phone is awkward.
  Suggest allowing a single-image system picker for Image fields, separately from the Photos source.
- **D-17, one Cloud project.** `drive.file` grants belong to the Google Cloud project. If the web, Android and
  iOS OAuth clients are in different projects, a sheet picked on one platform can't be written from another,
  and the S39 re-pick (A-158) will appear often. Keep every client in one project and verify in the spike.
- **D-17, repeat imports.** Under `drive.file`, the app may list the files the person already chose. A small
  "Spreadsheets you chose before" list on `S15-connected` would speed up repeat imports without a new scope.
  Not designed.
- **D-18.** If the Android 17 picker can't select in bulk, the picker is a poor fit for importing hundreds of
  contacts. The design falls back cleanly (A-155), but run the spike early.
- **D-13, dark mode.** Apple's rules make its button white on dark backgrounds while Google's stays dark, so on
  Android and web in dark mode Apple is the brightest option. Accepted; if equal prominence matters, switch
  Google to its light theme in dark mode too.
- **D-13, no name from Apple.** Apple may return no name, and members see names on shared lists. Consider
  asking for a name before the first share or join (S31/S33). Not designed.
- **D-08.** "Signing in keeps them" does not fit the one-line Home indicator next to "Back up"; it is said in
  the S05 web sheet, the S09 web line and the S27 nudge instead.
- **D-09 row 4.** A removal shown only as a snackbar is too easy to miss when decisions were lost, so it is an
  inline alert (plus the existing Activity event).
- **D-33.** Sign-in now offers "Choose lists" (A-135): without it, a person who signs in only to share one
  list would upload private lists they never meant to back up, which defeats D-33's purpose.
