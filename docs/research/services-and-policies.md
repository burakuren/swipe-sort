# SwipeSort: services and store-policy research

Every fact below was checked on 2026-10-10. A fact marked UNVERIFIED rests on third-party sources or on memory, not on a primary source.

## A. Google Play

1. **Closed testing for new personal accounts.** A personal developer account created after Nov 13, 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 consecutive days. At least 12 testers must still be opted in when you apply for production access. If a tester opts out and back in, the count restarts, because the 14 days must be consecutive. Production and pre-registration stay disabled until the requirement is met. Accounts created before Nov 13, 2023 are not covered, and organization accounts are exempt. The official page lists no 2025 or 2026 changes. The requirement was 20 testers until it was cut to 12 (third-party sources date the cut to Dec 2024, UNVERIFIED). Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465 (checked 2026-10-10)
2. **Android developer verification.** Verification opened to all developers in March 2026. Since the Play Console requirement update on July 15, 2026, Play developers must register their apps; 99% were registered automatically. The developer should check registration status on the Play Console Home page and register any remaining apps by **Sep 30, 2026**, or risk the app being removed from Google Play globally. From Sep 30, 2026, verification is enforced for users in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand. It expands globally to all certified devices in **2027**. Limited-distribution accounts (no ID and no fee, up to 20 devices) launched in Aug 2026. Unregistered apps can still be installed with adb or through an "advanced flow". Source: https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/06/android-developer-verification.html and https://developer.android.com/developer-verification (checked 2026-10-10). Whether the founder's account still has unregistered apps: UNVERIFIED (check the Play Console Home page).
3. **Target API level.** From Aug 31, 2026, new apps and app updates must target **API 36 (Android 16)**. Existing apps must target API 35 or higher to stay available to new users on newer OS versions. An extension to Nov 1, 2026 can be requested. The page gives no 2027 date; by the yearly pattern the next step would be API 37 around Aug 2027 (UNVERIFIED). Source: https://developer.android.com/google/play/requirements/target-sdk (checked 2026-10-10)
4. **Account deletion.** An app that lets users create an account must offer an **in-app path** to delete the account and its data. It must also provide a **web link** where users can request deletion, entered in the Play Console form; the deletion path must be prominent on that page. The data associated with the account must be deleted, including data declared in the Data safety form. Data held by service providers (backends) must also be deleted. Some data may be kept for security, fraud prevention or regulatory reasons, if users are told about it (for example in the privacy policy). Deactivating the account does not count as deletion. Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111 (checked 2026-10-10)
5. **Data safety form.** "Collect" means transmitting data off the device. Data processed only on the device is not collected and need not be declared. Passing data to another app on the device still counts as "sharing". Relevant data types: Contacts; Photos (separate from Videos); Email address (Personal info); Crash logs and Diagnostics (App info and performance). Ephemerally processed data must still be declared but is not shown in the listing. Transfers to service providers such as Supabase or Sentry are not "sharing". Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469 (checked 2026-10-10). For SwipeSort: contacts or photos synced to the backend count as collected.
6. **READ_CONTACTS.** This is now a **restricted permission with a minimum-scope alternative**. Apps targeting **API 37+** may request it only if the Android 17 **Contact Picker** can't support core functionality, and they must file a Play Console declaration. Timeline: announced Apr 15, 2026; declaration prompts start Sep 2026; compliance is mandatory from **Jan 27, 2027** for API 37+ targets, with a 30-day self-extension. Separately, non-public contacts are personal and sensitive data under the User Data policy. That policy requires an in-app **prominent disclosure** (not only in the listing), shown during normal use, describing the data and how it is used. It also requires **affirmative consent** (tap or checkbox) before any access; navigating away does not count as consent. Sources: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16935362 and https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311 (checked 2026-10-10). Contact Picker API details (ACTION_PICK_CONTACTS, Android 17+, multi-select): https://developer.android.com/about/versions/17/features/contact-picker (checked 2026-10-10, seen only through a localized search result). The claim that the older picker returns limited fields on API 36 and below is UNVERIFIED (third-party).
7. **Photos.** READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO are allowed only for apps whose **core functionality** needs broad photo access, such as gallery apps. Other apps must use the Android Photo Picker or another system picker. Apps that keep the permission must file a **Play Console declaration**. Compliance has been mandatory since May 28, 2025. Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14115180 (checked 2026-10-10)
8. **Privacy policy.** Every app needs a privacy-policy link in its designated Play Console field, plus a link or the text inside the app. The policy must sit at an active, public, non-geofenced URL (not a PDF), be clearly labeled as a privacy policy, and fully disclose data access, collection, use and sharing. Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311 (checked 2026-10-10)
9. **App Links.** Each host must serve `https://<host>/.well-known/assetlinks.json`. At least one intent filter needs `android:autoVerify="true"` with VIEW, BROWSABLE, DEFAULT and http/https. Every host, including each subdomain, is checked. On Android 15+, changes to the file can take up to 7 days to propagate. Source: https://developer.android.com/training/app-links/verify-android-applinks (checked 2026-10-10). Further rules: serve the file as application/json over HTTPS with no redirects, and include the Play App Signing key's SHA-256 (from the Play Console) plus the upload key's. These come from general knowledge of Google's docs and are UNVERIFIED on the page fetched.

## B. Google OAuth / Google Cloud

10. **Sign-in-only scopes (openid, email, profile).** These are not sensitive or restricted, so no scope verification is needed. The unverified-app screen and the **100-new-user cap** apply to apps requesting **sensitive or restricted** scopes before they are verified. **Brand verification** is needed for the app name and logo to appear on the consent screen; without it, only the domain is shown. It requires a homepage on a verified domain (Search Console), a privacy policy on the same domain and branding-guideline compliance, and it "usually completes in a few minutes". A published branding status is a prerequisite for requesting scope verification. Sources: https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/7454865 , https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/15549049 , https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13464321 (checked 2026-10-10)
11. **Scope classes.** `spreadsheets` and `spreadsheets.readonly` are **sensitive**. `drive.file` is **non-sensitive** and recommended. `drive.readonly` and `drive` are **restricted**. Source: https://developers.google.com/workspace/sheets/api/scopes (checked 2026-10-10). Sensitive verification needs a demo video showing the OAuth flow and scope use, a scope justification, a privacy policy and a homepage on authorized and verified domains. Source: https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13461325 (checked 2026-10-10). Restricted scopes need annual re-verification and a security assessment (CASA). Source: https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13463073 (checked 2026-10-10). Review time: the developer docs say 3–5 business days for sensitive scopes, while the Cloud FAQ says about 10 business days (https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/sensitive-scope-verification , https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13463817 ; seen in search snippets only, so UNVERIFIED). CASA cost and duration: UNVERIFIED.
12. **Google Picker on mobile.** The Picker now has a "desktop and mobile apps" mode. The OAuth request is sent with `trigger_onepick=true` (on Android: `AuthorizationRequest` with the `PICKER_OAUTH_TRIGGER` resource parameter). It opens in the system browser (embedded webviews are not allowed) and returns `picked_file_ids`. It allows **only drive.file**, which cannot be combined with other scopes. **Android and desktop are documented; iOS is not mentioned** (UNVERIFIED for iOS; a system-browser OAuth URL flow plausibly works there too). Each platform needs its own client ID. GA or preview status is not stated. Sources: https://developers.google.com/drive/picker/guides/overview and https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/picker/guides/desktop-mobile-picker (checked 2026-10-10). The web Picker (JS) works with drive.file in the Expo web build. Fallback with no Google scopes at all: let the user export the sheet as CSV or XLSX and import it as a file.
13. **@react-native-google-signin/google-signin.** The **free public version runs on the deprecated legacy Android Google Sign-In SDK** and supports Android and iOS only. The **paid "Universal Sign In" version** uses **Credential Manager**, adds web and macOS, installs from a private registry and needs a license. The price is listed on the vendor site, not in the docs (UNVERIFIED amount). Expo SDK 53+ meets its Android requirements. Source: https://react-native-google-signin.github.io/docs/install (checked 2026-10-10)

## C. Apple

14. **Apple Developer Program.** **US$99 per year** for individuals. Requirements: an Apple Account with 2FA, the age of majority, and the legal name, which is shown as seller. Paying with someone else's card delays enrollment and triggers an ID request. If no confirmation arrives within 24 hours of purchase, contact Apple; no other processing time is stated. Source: https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/ (checked 2026-10-10).
   - **Guideline 4.8:** an app that uses third-party or social login (Google, for example) for the primary account must **also offer an equivalent login service**. That service must limit data to name and email, let users hide their email, and not track for ads without consent. **Sign in with Apple is not named as mandatory**, but it is the obvious option that qualifies. If an app uses only its own email and password accounts, no additional login is needed. Source: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ (checked 2026-10-10)
   - **Guideline 5.1.1(v):** an app that supports account creation must let users **start account deletion in the app**. Deletion must cover the whole account and its personal data, including user-generated content shared with others; deactivating is not enough. Users must not be pushed to email or phone support. Guest or anonymous accounts must also be deletable. **Apps with Sign in with Apple should revoke user tokens through the Sign in with Apple REST API.** Source: https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/ (checked 2026-10-10)
15. **TestFlight.** Up to **10,000 external testers**, and invites can go out by public link. The first build added to an external group goes through **TestFlight App Review**. Builds expire after **90 days**. Sources: https://developer.apple.com/testflight/ and https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/overview-of-testflight/ (checked 2026-10-10). **Privacy nutrition labels** are required for every new app and update and are filled in App Store Connect. Data processed only on the device is not "collected". Third-party SDK practices (Sentry, analytics) must be included. Answers can be changed without an app update. Source: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/ (checked 2026-10-10)

## D. Backend and service pricing

16. **Supabase.**
   - **Free:** 2 active projects, which **pause after 1 week of inactivity**; 500 MB database per project; 50,000 MAU; 1 GB storage (50 MB max upload); 5 GB egress plus 5 GB cached; 500k Edge Function invocations; Realtime with 200 concurrent connections, 2M messages a month and 256 KB max message size.
   - **Pro:** **from $25/month**, with 100k MAU, 8 GB disk, 100 GB storage, 250 GB egress, 2M function invocations, 500 Realtime connections and 5M messages. Pro includes a $10 compute credit, which covers one Micro instance. Spend cap is on by default. Paid projects never pause.
   - **Compute add-ons:** Micro $10 (1 GB), Small $15 (2 GB), Medium $60 (4 GB), Large $110, and so on.
   - Source: https://supabase.com/pricing (checked 2026-10-10)
   - **Auth email:** the built-in sender only delivers to project team members, is limited to about 2 messages per hour, has no SLA, and is "not meant for production use". **Custom SMTP is required for production.** Source: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-smtp (checked 2026-10-10)
   - **Anonymous sign-in:** `signInAnonymously()` creates a user whose JWT carries an `is_anonymous` claim. The user is upgraded with `updateUser()` (email or phone, verified first) or `linkIdentity()` (OAuth); this needs **manual linking enabled**. Default limit: 30 requests per hour per IP. CAPTCHA or Turnstile is strongly recommended. Source: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-anonymous (checked 2026-10-10)
   - **Edge Functions:** 256 MB memory, 2 s CPU per request, 150 s wall clock on Free (400 s paid), 150 s idle timeout. Up to 100 functions on Free and 1,000 on Pro. Source: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/limits (checked 2026-10-10)
   - **supabase-js** has an official Expo quickstart. It uses the `expo-sqlite/localStorage` polyfill for sessions plus `react-native-url-polyfill`. Source: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/getting-started/quickstarts/expo-react-native (checked 2026-10-10). Web works because supabase-js is a browser library; this is general knowledge, not stated on that page.
17. **Firebase.**
   - **Spark (free):** Firestore with 1 GiB stored and 50k reads, 20k writes and 20k deletes per day; Auth with 50k MAU; Hosting with 10 GB stored and 360 MB/day transfer. **Cloud Functions and Cloud Storage are not available on Spark.**
   - **Blaze:** Cloud Functions include 2M free invocations a month. Storage includes 5 GB free (in us-central1, us-west1 and us-east1). Phone auth is billed per SMS.
   - Source: https://firebase.google.com/pricing (checked 2026-10-10). Cloud Storage, including default buckets, requires Blaze: https://firebase.google.com/docs/storage/faqs-storage-changes-announced-sept-2024 (checked 2026-10-10)
   - **Anonymous auth:** upgraded through `linkWithCredential`. With the Identity Platform upgrade, unlinked anonymous accounts can be cleaned up automatically after 30 days. Source: https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/web/anonymous-auth (checked 2026-10-10)
   - **React Native Firebase:** needs Expo dev builds and config plugins; it does **not** work in Expo Go. On web it falls back to the JS SDK, and the plain JS SDK is recommended for web. Crashlytics is native only. Source: https://rnfirebase.io/ (checked 2026-10-10)
18. **Convex.**
   - **Free:** 1M function calls a month, 0.5 GB database, 1 GB bandwidth, 1 GB file storage and 20 GB-hours of action compute.
   - **Professional:** 25M calls, 50 GB database, 50 GB bandwidth, 100 GB files.
   - Source: https://docs.convex.dev/production/state/limits (checked 2026-10-10). The Pro price of **$25 per developer per month** comes from third-party sources (UNVERIFIED; the pricing page did not render).
   - **PowerSync:** Free plan gives 2 GB synced a month, 500 MB hosted and 50 peak clients, and **deactivates after 1 week of inactivity**. Pro is **from $49/month** (30 GB synced, 10 GB hosted, 1,000 clients). Team is from $599. The Open Edition is free to self-host. Source: https://www.powersync.com/pricing (checked 2026-10-10)
   - **InstantDB: new Instant Cloud signups are closed. Cloud apps shut down on Aug 31, 2027**, after the team joined OpenAI; it remains open source for self-hosting. **Do not choose it.** Source: https://www.instantdb.com/pricing (checked 2026-10-10)
